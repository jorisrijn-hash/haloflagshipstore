Before you write

Can I try Halo without talking to anyone?

Yes, and we would rather you did. Under ten people it is free permanently, with no card details and no trial clock.

Do you do security reviews?

Yes. Send the questionnaire through the sales route and include your timeline. Most of what teams ask is already on the security page.

Can we self-host?

No. Halo is a hosted product. Workspace region is selectable, and full export is available on every plan.

How do I get a card that was sent by mistake removed?

The sender can delete their own card. If they have left, a workspace owner can. Ask support if neither applies.

Do you offer a discount for non-profits?

Ask through the sales route and tell us about the organisation.
