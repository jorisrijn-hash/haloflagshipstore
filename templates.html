Templates

Cards people are
glad to receive.

A template is not a theme. It is a piece of design made for one kind of moment, so that a two-line message still arrives looking like someone made an effort.

The standard library

Included on every plan, including free. 12 templates.

Premium collections

Optional. Bought with points by the person sending, or licensed for the whole workspace as an add-on. The standard library never expires or degrades.

Animated cards

The card face moves once, on open, and then holds still. A moment of light rather than a loop, so it survives being looked at more than once.

Included in the Premium templates add-on

Seasonal

Four releases a year. Retired designs stay valid on cards already sent.

Celebration packs

Larger formats for milestones a whole department signs.

Custom company templates

Your own designs, in your brand. Admins choose whether members can create them freely or whether new ones need approval first.
