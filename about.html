About

We are building one
thing, properly.

Halo is not an HR suite with a recognition module bolted on. It is a single object done well: a card that says what someone actually did, and stays.

Most recognition does not survive the week. Not because people do not care, but because appreciation has nowhere to live. A message scrolls away. A shout-out ends when the meeting does. An annual review arrives eleven months after the thing it describes. The feeling was real; the record was never made.

Mission

Help every company create a culture where appreciation is effortless, frequent and meaningful.

Vision

The most thoughtfully designed place where people feel seen, appreciated and connected at work. Not the biggest HR suite. The best one room in it.

Design principles

Eight words we
argue with every week.

Calm

Nothing on screen competes for attention with the message.

Elegant

Restraint first. If a flourish has to be justified, it goes.

Premium

It should feel like something, not like a form.

Human

A person wrote it. That is the only thing that makes it worth anything.

Minimal

Six fields on a card. Every one of them earns its place.

Editorial

Type does the work. Large, spacious, and set with intent.

Intentional

No feature exists because a competitor has it.

Timeless

A card sent today should still read well in five years.

What we are
deliberately not building.
