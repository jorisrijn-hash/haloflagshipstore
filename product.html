A room only your
company can enter.

Registration is tied to a verified company email domain. There is no public feed, no discovery, no cross-company social graph. What is said inside stays inside.

  • Domain-verified joining

    Only addresses on your domain can create an account in your workspace.

  • Your logo, your accent

    Workspace branding sits underneath Halo's, never on top of it.

  • Directory sync

    People, departments and managers arrive from Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or SCIM.

  • Approved templates

    Admins can curate which card designs are available and require approval for custom ones.

Who can do what.

Six roles, and the only meaningful difference between them is how much they can change and whether they need points to recognise someone.

The reward for being valued
is the ability to value someone else.

Receiving a card earns points. Points are not a currency you cash out, they are access: premium templates, animated cards, seasonal collections. Recognition circulates instead of terminating.

  • Managers and HR never spend them

    Nobody should run out of the ability to say thank you.

  • They carry over

    Points do not expire monthly. There is no artificial urgency.

  • Optional gifts

    If your company enables it, points can go toward a real reward. Most companies leave this off, and Halo works fine that way.

  • No quotas

    Halo will never tell someone they are behind on appreciation. That is how you get hollow recognition.
