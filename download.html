Before you begin

Sign in using your workspace email address and the password provided by your organization.

Halo accounts are managed by your company workspace. Personal email addresses cannot be used unless your administrator has enabled them.

  • Secure company authentication
  • End-to-end encrypted
  • Company managed access
Enterprise security

Your organization’s data stays protected with enterprise-grade security.

Company workspace

Every account belongs to a verified workspace, ensuring trust and control.

Automatic updates

Always stay on the latest version of Halo with automatic updates.

Can I install Halo on multiple devices?

Yes. One account works across as many machines as you use, and every card syncs to all of them.

How do I log in?

With the workspace email address your organization set up, and the password they issued. Single sign-on is available on Enterprise.

I forgot my password.

Use the reset link on the sign-in screen. If your workspace uses single sign-on, your identity provider handles it instead.

Can I use my personal email?

Only if your administrator has explicitly enabled it. By default Halo accounts are tied to a verified company domain.

Who manages my account?

Your workspace owner or HR admin. They control roles, visibility policies and who can join.
