The reward for being valued
is the ability to value someone else.

Receiving a card earns points. Points are not a currency you cash out, they are access: premium templates, animated cards, seasonal collections. Recognition circulates instead of terminating.

  • Managers and HR never spend them

    Nobody should run out of the ability to say thank you.

  • They carry over

    Points do not expire monthly. There is no artificial urgency.

  • Optional gifts

    If your company enables it, points can go toward a real reward. Most companies leave this off, and Halo works fine that way.

  • No quotas

    Halo will never tell someone they are behind on appreciation. That is how you get hollow recognition.
