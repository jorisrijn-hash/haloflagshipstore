Questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Plans change at any time and billing is prorated to the day. Downgrading never deletes cards that have already been sent.

Is there a minimum number of users?

No minimum. Under ten people Halo is free, and there is no seat commitment on any paid plan.

What happens to cards when someone leaves?

The workspace keeps the record. The person keeps a personal export of every card they received.

Do unused points roll over?

Points carry over month to month. Managers and HR can always send recognition without spending points.

Who can see a card?

Cards are private between sender and recipient by default. The sender can make a card visible to the team or the whole company, within the policy your workspace sets.

Do add-ons have to cover everyone?

Add-ons are priced per user and apply to the whole workspace, so the price you see is the number of paid seats multiplied by the add-on.
